The free tools (tools.shaikhismail.com)

Tools include PDF to Image, Image to PDF, PDF Merge, PDF Split, PDF Merge & Split, Image Compressor, Exam Photo Resizer, GST & EMI Calculator and Age Calculator, and any tools added later. They work like this:

  • No database. We do not run a database or file storage for customer data.

Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

The tools ask for a quick Cloudflare Turnstile check before they unlock. Cloudflare looks at technical signals from your browser, such as your IP address and browser details, to tell people from bots. It never sees your files or what you type. A small verification service we run on Cloudflare only passes the one-time token to Cloudflare for a yes or no and keeps nothing. Cloudflare handles its part under the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

Only your light or dark theme choice, saved in your browser's local storage if you use the theme toggle. It never leaves your device. We do not set tracking or advertising cookies.
