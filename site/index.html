~/approach

How I take an AI use case from idea to production.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Sit with the people doing the work, map every step and pick where AI or automation saves the most time.

  2. 02

    Design

    Choose the architecture, plan for messy data and decide where an AI check or a human review is needed. Estimate the running cost.

  3. 03

    Build

    Agents, retrieval and APIs, with tracing from day one so every model call can be inspected.

  4. 04

    Run

    Test on real cases, document the solution, hand over, and keep improving it as the process changes.
