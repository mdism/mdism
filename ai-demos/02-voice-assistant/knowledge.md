# Cleanroom Gowning Quick Guide (SYNTHETIC TRAINING EXAMPLE, NOT A REAL SOP)

Document ID: DEMO-SOP-001, Version 1.0. Written for a software demo only.

1. Remove all jewellery, watches and make-up before entering the gowning room.
2. Wash hands for at least 30 seconds and dry them with a lint-free towel.
3. Put on items in this order: hair cover, face mask, coverall, overshoes, then sterile gloves.
4. Check the gloves for tears. Replace any torn glove before entering the cleanroom.
5. Gowns are single use. Do not re-use a gown after leaving the cleanroom.
6. If a gown touches the floor during gowning, discard it and start again with a new one.
7. Record your entry in the cleanroom logbook with date, time and your signature.
8. Report any deviation from this guide to the area supervisor on the same shift.
